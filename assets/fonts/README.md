These two small font files are subsets of **Noto Serif SC** (400 and 600),
distributed under the SIL Open Font License in `OFL-NotoSerifSC.txt`.
Source: `@fontsource-variable/noto-serif-sc` version 5.3.1, sourced from Google Fonts.

The subsets are renamed **Scholar Serif** and contain Latin characters, the
profile names, and Chinese section headings used by the red palette. Other
characters fall back to the visitor's installed Chinese serif font.
The combined WOFF2 size is about 41 KB. The fonts are served locally and are only
requested when the red palette uses them.
