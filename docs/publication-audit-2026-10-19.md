## Decisions

- Collective DeepSeek reports display `DeepSeek-AI` with an explicit
  `including Shaofei Cai` note and a link to the full author list.
- V4 Pro/Flash and V4.1-Flash/1017 releases are represented by their respective
  shared reports; model variants are not separate publications.
- PGT and ROCKET-2 replace their earlier entries rather than duplicating them.
- Published works use the publisher's publication date. SPEAR uses its verified
  conference presentation date. LoopNav's sorting date records the date the
  accepted listing was checked, not a future proceedings publication date.
- SmartSnap and ROCKET-3 remain 2025 preprints in the verified sources. A 2026
  revision alone does not establish a 2026 publication. SmartSnap was not added
  in this update because it falls outside the requested year range.
- The inspected V3, R1 (including the latest PDF), DeepSeekMath-V2 and Engram
  reports do not contain `Shaofei Cai` in their author lists.

This update therefore uses arXiv, publisher and conference records, with ORCID
and OpenAlex used to find candidates. It is not a complete reconciliation of the
Google Scholar profile. Loose arXiv author-search matches were checked for the
exact author identity before inclusion.
